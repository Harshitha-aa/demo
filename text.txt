# NextStep

### *Before you take the next step, learn from people who already did.*

***

# Overview

**NextStep** is a platform where people share the real outcomes of important life decisions.

Every day, people face questions like:

* Should I switch careers?
* Should I move abroad?
* Should I join a startup?
* Should I pursue higher studies?
* Should I learn a certain technology?
* Should I quit my current job?
* Should I start my own business?

Today, most people look for answers through:

* Random YouTube videos
* Reddit discussions
* Quora answers
* Influencers
* Friends and family

The problem is that most advice is based on opinions.

**NextStep is built around experiences, not opinions.**

***

# Core Idea

Instead of asking:

> "What do you think I should do?"

Users ask:

> "Who has already done this, and what happened?"

The platform becomes a collection of real-life journeys, experiences, decisions, successes, mistakes, and lessons.

***

# Decision Updates

One unique feature of NextStep.

A journey does not end after posting.

People can revisit their decision.

Example:

***

## January

"I resigned from my job."

***

## After 3 Months

"I joined a startup."

***

## After 6 Months

"Best decision I've made."

***

## After 1 Year

"Here's what happened."

***

Every experience becomes a living timeline.

***
